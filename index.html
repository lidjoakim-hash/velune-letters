Questions

Before you send

Do guests need to download anything?

No. The link opens in any phone or computer browser.

Can I change details after sending?

Yes. Changes appear for every guest straight away, so a new start time never needs a second invitation.

How do I see who is coming?

Every reply lands in your guest list with party size, dinner choice and notes. You can export it for the venue.

Can different guests see different languages?

With Signature, guests switch between English, Română, Norsk, Italiano, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português, Svenska, Dansk, Polski and Nederlands inside the invitation. Their phone's language opens first.

What about grandparents without a smartphone?

Atelier includes a printable card in the same design, with a QR code to the invitation.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. Design the whole invitation in your names for free and preview it on your phone. You only pay when you publish and send it to guests.

I have a question that isn't here

Send us a message on Instagram at @lettersbyvelune. We usually reply within a few hours.

How long does the invitation stay online?

Until 12 months after your wedding. After the day, it can become a thank-you page.
